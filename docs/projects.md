# Key Projects

## Semi-Dirac spin liquids and frustrated quantum magnetism on the trellis lattice

**Ph.D. project**

* Fully symmetric PSG classification using Abrikosov fermions
* Identified 256 *Z₂* and 448 *U(1)* algebraic PSGs
* Reduced to symmetry-allowed mean-field Ansätze
* Computed static and dynamical structure factors

📄 **Publication:**
[Phys. Rev. Research 8, 013191 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1103/xmtj-4kj9) · [arXiv:2509.02663](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02663)

**Keywords:** Quantum Spin Liquids, Projective Symmetry Group

## From dimensional reduction to tetramerization in mixed ferro-antiferro breathing pyrochlores

**Research project**

* Studied the spin-1/2 nearest-neighbor Heisenberg model on the breathing pyrochlore lattice in the mixed ferro-antiferromagnetic regime
* Used pseudofermion functional renormalization group (PFFRG) to investigate the nonmagnetic regime and tetramer correlations
* Connected the theoretical phase diagram with density-functional-theory parameters for breathing chromium thiospinels

📄 **Preprint:**
[arXiv:2609.30162](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30162)


**Keywords:** Breathing Pyrochlores, Frustrated Magnetism, PFFRG, DMRG, Tetramerization
